Privacy Policy

Application: Test Data Lab · Last updated: May 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the Test Data Lab application ("the Application") handles information. The Application is a locally hosted, single-operator tool used for personal support and testing. It is not offered to external end users.

1. Information the Application Accesses

  • QuickBooks Online company data accessed via Intuit's API after you authorize a connection — for example customers, vendors, items, accounts, and transactions.
  • OAuth tokens issued by Intuit to maintain the authorized connection.
  • Operator account information such as a login email used to sign in to the Application.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to operate the Application's features: connecting to authorized companies, generating and inspecting test data, reproducing support scenarios, creating checkpoints, and producing support analysis and notes.

3. Where Information Is Stored

The Application runs locally and stores data — including OAuth tokens and any retrieved company data — in a database controlled by the operator. Data is not hosted on a shared public service by this Application.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Intuit / QuickBooks Online is the source of company data and the OAuth provider. Your use of QuickBooks Online is governed by Intuit's own privacy and terms.
  • AI provider. When AI-assisted features are used, relevant company data may be transmitted to the configured AI provider (for example Anthropic or OpenAI) for the sole purpose of generating the requested analysis or support notes. This data is subject to that provider's privacy terms.

5. Information Sharing

The Application does not sell information and does not share it with third parties except the services described above that are required to provide its functionality.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

The operator controls retention. Authorized connections can be disconnected at any time, after which stored tokens are revoked, and locally stored company data can be deleted by the operator.

7. Security

Access to the Application is protected by authentication, and OAuth tokens are stored for the purpose of maintaining authorized connections. Because the Application is operator-hosted, the operator is responsible for securing the host environment.

8. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.

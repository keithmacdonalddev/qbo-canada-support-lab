# Bulk business activity

Company → Bulk business activity prepares the published continuing business schedule through an elapsed date, then runs it as a durable background job. Preparation imports exact saved native identities and preserves their original creation receipts; it does not create QBO records. The owner confirms the prepared remaining count and Production company before Start. Stop drains an admitted batch; Resume retains saved identities.

The worker uses dependency frontiers of at most30 independent creates per QBO batch. Shared originating records and stock mutations for the same product run in separate cohorts. It groups full reads by entity/ID, coalesces master reads and briefly caches static definitions. Batches start at least two seconds apart, below Intuit's40batch/minute ceiling. The limit and30-operation guidance are documented in [Intuit's batch sample](https://github.com/IntuitDeveloper/SampleApp-Batch-Java/blob/main/docs/building-smarter-batch.md).

A CompanyWriter delegation fences normal writers and the original idle run without resetting its cursor. Every batch is reconstructed from approved immutable intents inside native write admission, with current company authority, protected-period checks and original parent receipts. Native dispatch/result audits retain exact request bytes and per-item response correlation. Unknown outcomes hold the writer; they are never automatically replayed. Known accepted siblings retain their IDs. An explicit owner Retry action can reset only exact native HTTP400 create rejections after replaying their batch item and dispatch/result audits. It archives each failed compilation/dispatch before an atomic reset, caps retained retries at two, and recompiles against fresh source availability. Saved or uncertain identities remain ineligible. A prepared, proved never-sent cohort can be resumed.

Creation/content checking is distinct from full relationship verification. The known omitted Estimate→Invoice conversion remains an explicit coverage gap. Exact money, quantities, tax, posting accounts, actual settlements and source consumption are checked independently. This workflow never advances the verified business calendar, certifies reports or claims bank reconciliation. Version1 supports the core nine-entity continuing schedule; credits and other lifecycle cases retain separate workflows.

Implementation: business-population-service/runtime/plan/reader/batch/receipt/proof modules, BusinessPopulationJob/Record models, authenticated /api/business-population routes and the Company panel. New collections/indexes are prepared only by explicit Prepare, with no startup migration. The server scanner resumes running/stopping jobs after lease expiry.

Focused evidence covers all nine financial record types, grouped/coalesced reads, exact wire/manifest integrity, native batch settlement, lost acknowledgements, duplicate/missing response identities and Stop/Resume. Live results belong in the current execution handoff; fixture success alone is not live completion.

Linked Canadian Deposits set GlobalTaxCalculation to NotApplicable and identify the entire Payment using TxnLineId0. The [official Deposit reference](https://developer.intuit.com/app/developer/qbo/docs/api/accounting/all-entities/deposit) requires the tax-calculation field for non-US companies.

Population GETs share three slots,180ms spacing and a330-per-minute rolling budget, including protected-period and Deposit-availability reads. Controlled GET429s wait65seconds and retry at most twice; a fully unsent, settled read-throttled job can continue automatically after its saved retry time. Accounting POSTs retain their one-attempt/no-unknown-replay boundary. Repeated financial checks reuse already observed cohort data and posting routes; final writer-fence and transactional admission checks remain mandatory.

A retained exact Invoice→TimeActivity link may coexist with provider TimeActivity.BillableStatus still Billable. Financial creation records time-invoice-billing-unverified separately; it still requires the exact customer, item, duration, quote economics and creation receipts. Both Estimate and TimeActivity are reserved against a second managed Invoice, regardless of provider billing status. Native full graph verification is unchanged.

## Relationship issue workflow

Company → Bulk business activity groups outstanding Estimate conversion, time billing and failed/unchecked-record checks. The owner can browse up to25 exact records per page, inspect the retained expected/observed states and links, open the matching canonical entity in Records or QuickBooks, read the resolution guidance, and document an evidence-bound limitation. A documented limitation stays visible and unverified; changed record/neighbor evidence invalidates the disposition. Bulk creation origin in Records is correlated to the original per-item batch request/accepted response and native dispatch/result audits. It grants no edit permission.

Recheck relationships queues a separate durable read-only worker with a cursor, lease, progress, Stop and Resume. It continues without the page or an AI session. Queues are grouped by transaction date; bounded bidirectional saved-record closure includes payments and deposits so paid source transactions can be checked correctly. Reads use the existing shared rate queue and an exact-ID/GET-only adapter. Every group is fenced against current company access and competing company creation/recheck jobs. Snapshot/receipt/content failures preserve earlier warnings and surface a failure issue. A failed or stopped run resumes its retained queue; a completed run can check remaining warnings again. Each request, group, control action and completion has a scoped audit. Rechecks never edit accounting records or advance verifiedThrough.

Clearing conversion warnings requires replayed original financial content, receipt and policy plus strict native relationships: exact invoice/estimate/time identities, actual header links, reciprocal Closed estimate, billed time and matching economics/tax. Original whole-estimate requests use the existing strict whole-estimate verifier. Original legacy line-link requests additionally require their exact original physical line link and source line; no compilation is rewritten or relabelled. Relationship success retains a hashed/audited validation-completion clock so later source/report observations can be replayed honestly. Proof, audit and financial replacements are atomic in writer-fenced chunks of at most25 records.

Newly prepared jobs use relationshipPolicyVersion1. The first estimate-to-invoice pattern is limited to one native trial. After strict native success, normal cohorts of up to30 are allowed; every new cohort must receive strict relationship readback before a further matching cohort. Admission consults the same authority's records across jobs/restarts and rejects prior saved/dispatched/unknown conversions without proved success. A failed pattern pauses its dependent branch while independent later-month activity continues. createdThrough remains behind the first incomplete month. Existing completed population jobs are preserved.

Authenticated actions under /api/business-population are POST /:id/issues (bounded read-only issue list), /:id/recheck, /:id/recheck/stop and /:id/limitation. Requests accept only the exact listed fields; company/identity/status overrides are rejected. Recheck and limitation actions are owner-only. Public responses contain bounded evidence summaries, never raw source packets or credentials. No new collection or startup migration is introduced.

These controls manage evidence and documented exceptions. They do not silently repair existing records, recreate transactions, certify reports or complete bank reconciliation. Rendered live warning/record inspection and isolated fixture rechecks are separate evidence; no new live recheck or accounting repair is implied by this implementation.

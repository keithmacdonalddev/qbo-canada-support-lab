# Governed business lifecycle version 2

Scope, 2026-10-15: make a newly published business produce plausible working capital and operating costs, while preserving existing immutable version-1 definitions, calendar identities, receipts, policies and follow-ups. This is QBO mutation-path implementation, not permission to run it on the connected company.

Version selection belongs to the saved business definition. Missing version continues to mean version 1, with its original canonical definition and generated transaction details. Newly proposed businesses select version 2 visibly; owners can keep the original version before publication. Published authority must agree with the exact saved template version and rules hash. New v2 identities use harbour-pine-v2. This unit does not replace or amend an already active business; future-effective replacement needs its own reviewed design preserving old obligations.

Version 2 keeps the same three operating divisions and fixture price proposals, and adds three nonbillable monthly overhead bills (CAD 950 premises, 180 utilities, and 95 software before observed purchase tax), each with its own CAD supplier and operating-expense account line. Bills receive an explicit 30-day due date and are paid from the selected bank 20 days later. Stock bills retain purchase-order conversion. Stock sales keep six units from each opening-month batch as a continuing buffer; later batches buy the planned sales quantity. This intentionally creates a bounded opening buffer rather than growing stock every month. Stock orders in the same fixed exception cohorts are paid at 45 days. Service hours vary deterministically by season; quotes, time and invoices share the same occurrence economics.

The immutable exception cohorts are 001, 006, 011 and so on (zero-based index divisible by five), consistently at both volumes. Selected field jobs collect 60% of the actual accepted taxed invoice at day 21 and its exact remainder at day 45, with separate bank deposits the following day. Other field jobs keep one full settlement. Selected care customers pay at day 45 instead of day 14; all new invoices state a 30-day due date. No future follow-up may be dispatched. The nominal monthly record target is 80 for development and 278 for flagship; dates at period edges can change observed monthly totals. These v2 targets are explicitly separate from the historical owner-approved v1 targets 70/260.

The compiler must fail closed on partial settlement unless its exact saved policy names the installment fraction or prior payment. A final payment depends on the original invoice AND the exact earlier managed payment; current verified balances, party, currency, destination and line application must agree. It must never infer a remainder from an arbitrary current balance. The final request creates a payment only; no existing record edits, voids, deletions, external payment processing or raw API tool is added. Extra prerequisite links are not advertised as accounting links. Fresh graph verification must continue to reconcile invoice balance to all managed settlements.

Account-based expense bills use the existing Bill/BillPayment transport, current chosen Expense/AP/tax references, before-tax lines, server-derived dated Canadian tax policy and full saved readback. No customer/project, billable expense, payroll, retainer or invented journal is introduced. The sale compiler accepts a reduced opening batch only under the v2 explicit quantity contract; legacy complete-batch rules remain unchanged. Independent cost/cash/stock expectations must verify outputs in a normal publication -> prepare -> approve -> original SDK -> saved graph -> reports -> adjacent calendar fixture, including retained partial balances before final settlement and buffer stock after rollover.

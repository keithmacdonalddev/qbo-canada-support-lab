# Managed stock costs

The business can require its stock purchases, remaining stock and cost of sales to agree before its calendar advances. This is an optional requirement on a newly published business. It adds evidence to existing purchases and sales; it creates no new accounting operation or collection.

## Recorded method and tax basis

The saved business retains FIFO (oldest purchases are consumed first) with either an owner observation or an explicitly labelled documented inference, its source reference and evidence date. An inference remains an inference in the draft and publication screen; it is never relabelled an owner observation. A separate reference/date records the basis for choosing fully recoverable purchase tax, which adds no product cost. Delegated fictional-business choices must be identified as such rather than as facts about filed returns. Publication shows the recorded basis and binds it to the exact review. Removing or changing the draft clears that review and consent. Period comparisons retain the policy hash and report their reliance on method/tax policy evidence; passing amounts do not certify a directly observed company setting.

Unknown or moving-average methods, capitalized/nonrecoverable purchase tax, fractional stock, ambiguous unequal-cost transactions on the same day, negative stock, adjustments and returns remain unavailable. Neither company country nor a missing preference field proves FIFO.

## An original zero starting point

All five stock product identities remain in the published business, including dormant products. An eligible root requires the original app-created Item request, successful original transport/audits, verified zero readback and unchanged original version. Current complete Item metadata must show the same zero quantities, account routing and original starting date, with equal creation/update timestamps. An adopted record, owner-attributed master, later Item edit or missing original receipt cannot establish this root.

The metadata-preserving mode of the existing inventory reader retains two complete matching scans of supported current records. It bounds actual GET admissions at 240, retained product data at 100 Items/2.5 MB and the admission interval at three minutes. It uses the original scoped client, stops admissions on failure and drains both scan workers, including original SDK/token work. The old compact reader contract remains unchanged. This is observed stability, not an atomic snapshot or proof about every unsupported QuickBooks activity.

Other Inventory Items may coexist only on known separate asset and cost-of-sales accounts. Selected opening inventory value must be zero. The root retains original master receipts, zero Item observations, the accepted baseline, account definitions, policy and observed company fiscal calendar. The owner's publication hash binds all of them. No existing business can silently acquire this requirement later.

## Period preparation and evidence

A period freezes either the accepted zero root or the exact immediately preceding committed closing state. It revalidates the original remaining purchase content and current quantities. Starting inventory value must equal the carried purchase layers; prior drift cannot become a new accepted opening. Observed company fiscal-year rollover resets accumulated cost of sales while preserving the stock layers. A changed fiscal-year starting month refuses new work.

The pure calculator processes original accepted Bill and SalesReceipt lines in accounting-date order. Each purchase layer retains its exact physical product, whole quantity, unit cost in cents, operation, logical line, compilation, request and original receipt hashes. It never derives expected cost from current PurchaseCost, sales price or reported cost of sales. Multiple same-day sales that would require choosing between unequal-cost layers refuse. Evidence and allocation counts have finite limits.

The original seven-report checks and selected financial movements remain required. The separate stock-cost assertion compares managed closing value and accumulated cost of sales to the two selected trial-balance accounts, and compares each selected Item quantity. A balanced but incorrect cost posting keeps the period incomplete. The final proof retains original accepted stock lines, closing layers, the observation and the comparison in the existing evidence/calendar transaction.

Future business revisions retain the original policy, all physical stock identities and the exact root. New plans cannot omit the published requirement. Existing immutable plans and financial proof versions 1–3 retain their original bytes and recovery rules.

## Recovery and limits

Stop/continue recomputes from the same frozen opening and accepted rows. It does not mutate a consumption counter or repeat accepted purchases. Owner identification of a lost-response purchase retains the original unknown transport receipt and labels that provenance in the cost proof. An owner-attributed master still cannot establish the original zero root. Pending calendar completion repairs from the original retained evidence without a new company-eligibility read. Historical inspection uses its original observation.

Before the first create, the existing proof-capacity check reserves space for both account and stock evidence under JSON and BSON limits. The complete retained row is checked again before storage. Failure or missing original evidence cannot advance the calendar. The desktop shows passed, failed and unavailable states while preserving operation progress and Stop.

This is a bounded prediction from managed original purchase layers and a comparison of selected accounts and quantities. The costing method and recoverable-tax treatment rely on the retained owner observation. It does not certify independent QuickBooks per-item valuation, physical counts, all ledger activity, bank reconciliation or a believable complete company. Actual connected-company eligibility and acceptance remain unverified.

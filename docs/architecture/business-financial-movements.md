# Expected financial movements for managed business periods

Scope, 2026-10-15: prove that new governed operations change the selected bank, undeposited funds, receivables and payables accounts by their exact planned amounts. A balanced trial balance and an income total do not establish that these movements are right. This unit adds accounting evidence to the existing prepare/approve/run/calendar path; it does not authorize live company actions.

New normal plans retain their exact CAD accrual trial balance for the operation's end date, selected account definitions, source-observation hash, and signed starting account balances. A new financial-movements assertion is mandatory for these plans. Existing immutable plans and completed proofs retain their original requirements; no automatic migration or replacement occurs. Before the first unsent dispatch the same four starting balances must still match, alongside existing seven-report and income guards.

After fresh graph verification, expected account deltas come from each new record's original verified compiled create request and original owner-approved Canadian tax policy at its dispatch. Tax expectations must bind the actual policy hash retained on the dispatch, including the exact historical amendment head; expected tax never comes from the report being checked or the saved transaction's tax value. Installments use the exact original compiled payment amount, not today's outstanding balance. Earlier-period dependencies are excluded from new-period deltas. Estimates, purchase orders and time entries have zero posting effect. Every create is represented exactly once.

Use signed debit-minus-credit balances by exact account ID, resolved from unique Debit/Credit columns rather than positional assumptions. Require a complete unfiltered server-owned trial balance, exact period/end date, CAD accrual basis, unique numeric account identities, supported cells and matching total debits/credits. An omitted account can mean zero only in this validated complete report with its selected observed account definition; a mapped header/summary-only account or any ambiguous detail fails closed. Do not use the Account entity's present CurrentBalance as a historical baseline.

Invoice: increase receivables by planned net plus approved tax. Sales receipt: increase selected bank by the same gross amount. Bill: increase payable credit by planned gross. Purchase-tax recoverability is not proved by the present TaxCode/TaxRate source, so exact expense-account movement is deferred until that separate posting contract is observed or explicitly governed. Payment: decrease receivables and increase its explicit bank/undeposited destination. Bill payment: decrease payable credit and selected bank. Deposit: move the exact linked payment amount from undeposited funds to selected bank. Reject unsupported endpoints, accounts, currencies or line/settlement shapes rather than silently skipping them. The current compiler/readback remains authoritative for the exact managed source/party/content links.

Keep original compiled requests and full policies in their existing original immutable intent/dispatch stores. Retain a bounded normalized basis identifying each original create, compilation/request/policy hashes, exact amendment head, accepted record ID and signed four-account postings with the period proof. Batch-read original current-operation Steps and Intents and rederive every posting using the exact dispatch policy head; do not duplicate full compiled requests or TaxRate histories in period proof. Validate it against the original manifest's create set, original dispatch compilation/request/policy hashes, and saved record identity when loading retained proof. Final persistence still requires the same current actor, lease, company writer fence and fresh exact graph. A failure leaves the calendar unadvanced and cannot replay creates. Retain and display successful, failed and unavailable four-account comparisons through normal operation inspection, including starting amounts, expected changes, expected/actual endings, differences, scoped source hash and observation time. Incomplete immutable observations increment the existing evidence revision but cannot set a completion candidate. Never replace the starting snapshot after this operation has sent a create. Before the first actual create, revalidate opening balances even after dependency-only ordinal progress and resume.

This is selected balance-sheet-account movement verification, not exact expense posting, complete inventory valuation, FIFO cost, tax-control-account reconciliation, statement-of-cash-flows reconciliation, bank-statement reconciliation, original-case realism or live Canadian acceptance. Inventory cost cannot be assumed from an item's current zero quantity when backdated unmanaged activity may consume earlier lots. Preserve these limits visibly. No new QBO writes, record edits, voids, deletions, journal entries, raw AI tools, indexes, startup jobs or credential changes are introduced.

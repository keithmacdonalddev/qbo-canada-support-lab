A calm operating surface for a believable, continuing business.

This guide turns the rebuild plan into a buildable interface language: scope before content, outcome before metrics, preview before mutation, and evidence beside every important claim.

Accepted direction, not live UI. These examples use fixtures, make no network requests, and do not authorize Production or database operations.

Six rules shape the system

The interface borrows a reasoning method—purpose, agency, responsibility, familiarity, flexibility, and craft—without imitating another product or platform.

01 · SCOPE

Scope before content

Company, environment, business date, active work, and role stay visible.

02 · OUTCOME

Outcome before metrics

Readiness and attention lead. Numbers explain the decision instead of competing with it.

03 · AGENCY

Preview before mutation

Consequential work names its effect, confirmation, safe stop, and recovery boundary.

04 · EVIDENCE

Claims show their proof

Coverage includes source, observation time, owner, status, and limitation.

05 · CONTEXT

Unified, not uniform

Desktop stays precise and dense; narrow views prioritize review and safe control.

06 · CRAFT

Opaque data planes

Stable surfaces, restrained motion, deliberate geometry, and legible financial data.

Content makes consequence plain

Name the action

Prefer “Start approved August backfill” over “Run” and “Stop after current safe step” over “Cancel”.

Lead with outcome

Say what happened, then explain cause, evidence, and a valid recovery path.

Keep uncertainty

Unknown, not observed, stale, partial, and manual only are product states—not copy failures.

Protect diagnostics

Show sanitized request IDs and Intuit transaction IDs when useful. Never show tokens, keys, raw authorization, or secrets.

Accessibility is a component contract

Designed in

Skip links, landmarks, labelled controls, visible focus, heading order, text-plus-colour status, 44px narrow targets, local table scrolling, live-region restraint, and reduced-motion support.

Still requires live acceptance

Manual keyboard walkthrough, NVDA, forced colours, 200% zoom, focus restoration, automated accessibility tests, and production-route state coverage after React migration.

Migration happens in reversible slices

1 · Foundations

Tokens, shell, scope, page header, buttons, status, alerts, and state primitives behind a feature flag.

2 · Read only

Overview, Coverage, and reports with fixture-to-route adapters and legacy routes preserved.

3 · App authority

Blueprint drafts only after server-owned permissions are approved.

4 · Consequential work

Preview and monitor precede any new executor. Records and Close follow as bounded vertical slices.
